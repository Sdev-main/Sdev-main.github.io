## Favorite Labs from CPSC-120L

* Lab 6

  Before Lab 6, I never fully understood blackjack or how you played it. After going through Lab 6, I came to realize how much
fun blackjack can be and the number of specifications needed to make it in a digital form. When I read the README.md file for
The first time, I thought this lab would be easy and simple to make. But after spending many hours and time to make
sure it was free of errors and mistakes, I began to take a different approach to the way I came prepared for both labs and lectures.
After this lab, I began to write much more thorough notes as well as many more examples of how certain functions worked. This lab
soon became a favorite of mine purely out of the work ethic that I was able to teach myself during my time working on it.
 
* Lab 8
  
  While not being my favorite lab, Lab 8 was the most memorable for me. Both before and after the lab, I dedicated a lot of my
notebook to taking notes on how the program behaved, as well as notes on important topics like the function std::stod(). This lab
was a tougher challenge for me, but it helped me learn the most and helped me get a much greater understanding of how every function
worked. Without the amount of time and dedication put into this lab, future labs would've become incredibly difficult for me.

* Lab 12

  Out of all the labs done in 120L, Lab 12 was my absolute favorite. Featuring Magick and the ability to use animated GIFs was something
I never thought I would get to experience during my first semester as a Computer Engineering student. With the utilization of vectors from
all the past labs, it was amazing to see all the work from students and professors combined put into the final lab. While the second part
of Lab 12 was a bit confusing, I'm glad to have gotten the ability to make not only animated pictures, but also the ability to create randomization
in programs.
